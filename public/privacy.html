Flowtree privacy

Flowtree is a personal planning tool. It stores your plan in your own Google Drive only after you choose to sign in.

Google Drive access

Flowtree requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This allows it to create, read, and update the Flowtree backup file that it owns in your Drive. It does not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, bank accounts, or files outside the scope Google grants to this app.

Your data

Your plan is stored in a private gpt folder in your Google Drive as flowtree-plan.json. On the Cloudflare-hosted version, requested plan data passes through the Flowtree backend to Google Drive but is not persisted on that backend. Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted and stored in Cloudflare Durable Objects to maintain sign-in. An HttpOnly session cookie identifies your session and expires after 30 days. The server operator controls the runtime and encryption key. The browser-only version uses short-lived Google access tokens in browser memory and session storage instead.

Control

You can sign out at any time. On the Cloudflare-hosted version, signing out deletes the corresponding server session and stored credentials; it does not delete your plan or sign out other devices. You can revoke Flowtree access in your Google account and remove the backup file directly from Drive. Browser preferences such as light or dark mode are stored on your device.
