Flowtree privacy

Flowtree is a personal planning tool. It stores your plan in your own Google Drive only after you choose to sign in.

Google Drive access

Flowtree requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This allows it to create, read, and update the Flowtree backup file that it owns in your Drive. It does not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, bank accounts, or files outside the scope Google grants to this app.

Your data

Your plan is stored in a private gpt folder in your Google Drive as flowtree-plan.json. A browser-local copy may be used as a resilient cache. Google authorization tokens are held only in memory and are not stored by Flowtree.

Control

You can disconnect Google Drive from Flowtree at any time and can remove the backup file directly from your Drive.
